CLASSICAL TRADITIONS

The Eight Classical Forms

Rooted in centuries of tradition, India's classical dance forms combine movement, rhythm, expression and storytelling.

01

Bharatanatyam

Grace • Rhythm • Expression

A highly expressive classical form known for precise movements, rhythmic footwork and powerful storytelling.

02

Kathak

Storytelling • Rhythm • Elegance

Famous for graceful spins, intricate footwork and storytelling through expressive movement.

03

Kathakali

Drama • Expression • Theatre

Kerala's spectacular dance-drama tradition, recognised for elaborate costumes and powerful expressions.

04

Kuchipudi

Grace • Drama • Rhythm

A dynamic dance tradition from Andhra Pradesh blending expressive storytelling, rhythmic movement and theatre.

05

Odissi

Sculptural • Graceful • Devotional

Known for its elegant poses, lyrical movement and distinctive tribhangi body posture.

06

Manipuri

Devotional • Soft • Flowing

A graceful tradition from Manipur, celebrated for fluid movements and devotional themes.

07

Mohiniyattam

Feminine • Grace • Flow

Kerala's graceful classical tradition characterised by gentle movements and elegant expressions.

08

Sattriya

Devotion • Rhythm • Spirituality

Assam's classical tradition developed through the Vaishnavite monastic culture of the region.

FOLK TRADITIONS

The Spirit of India

India's folk dances reflect the celebrations, stories, communities and everyday life of different regions across the country.

01

Bhangra

Punjab • Energy • Celebration

A vibrant Punjabi folk dance filled with energetic movements and celebratory rhythms.

02

Garba

Gujarat • Celebration • Rhythm

A circular community dance traditionally associated with festivals and joyful celebrations.

03

Ghoomar

Rajasthan • Grace • Celebration

A graceful Rajasthani folk dance known for flowing movements and beautiful traditional costumes.

04

Lavani

Maharashtra • Rhythm • Expression

A dynamic Maharashtrian performance tradition combining expressive movement, rhythm and music.

05

Dandiya

Gujarat • Sticks • Festival

A lively Gujarati dance performed with decorated sticks and rhythmic group patterns.

06

Bihu

Assam • Joy • Festival

An energetic Assamese folk tradition associated with seasonal celebrations and community life.
