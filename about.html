THE STORY OF SAMRA

Rooted in Tradition.

OUR STORY

Where Tradition Lives

Samra Dance Academy was envisioned as a space where India's rich dance heritage could be learned, experienced and passed forward with pride.

We believe Indian dance is much more than movement. It is a language of emotion, rhythm, storytelling and cultural identity.

From the precision of classical dance to the vibrant energy of folk traditions, Samra celebrates the extraordinary diversity of India's artistic heritage.

MEET THE FOUNDER

The Heart
Behind Samra

Samra
Founder & Artistic Director

Samra Dance Academy was born from a deep appreciation for India's beautiful dance traditions and a desire to create a place where those traditions could continue to inspire future generations.

Through dedication, discipline and love for the performing arts, the academy aims to guide students beyond technique — helping them discover confidence, expression and respect for cultural heritage.

Every student who walks through our doors becomes part of a larger story — one that connects the past with the future through the language of dance.
